## Recording rules

- Add one row per distributable asset, including generated illustrations and
  third-party libraries' image assets.
- Do not register real-site photos, user history, exports, screenshots of
  third-party services, or unapproved sample data as distributable assets.
- Attach or link the supporting permission/licence evidence in the release
  record; this table is an index, not proof by itself.
